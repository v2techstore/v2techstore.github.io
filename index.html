Privacy Policy

V2TECH Poster — Last updated: November 16, 2026

V2TECH Poster is an internal tool used by V2TECH, a laptop shop in Bab Ezzouar, Algiers, Algeria, to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account (@v2_tech).

What data we access

When the V2TECH account owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives:

  • Basic TikTok account information (display name, profile picture, account ID), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos, used only to post V2TECH's own content to V2TECH's own account.

How we use it

The data is used only to publish videos that the account owner has chosen and approved. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the V2TECH computer that runs the tool. They are not uploaded to any server or database run by us.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API.

Other users

The app is used only by V2TECH. It does not collect data from any other TikTok users.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions. Deleting the tool from the computer removes all stored tokens.
